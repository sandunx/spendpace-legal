Common questions

Where is my data?

On the phone, in this app only. There is no SpendPace website holding a copy.

I deleted the app.

The entries were on the device. They are not recoverable unless you exported a CSV first.

How do I delete everything?

Profile → Erase everything. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

The app is not charging me.

Version 1.0 is free. Paid plans are not turned on.

A reminder did not arrive.

Check that notifications are allowed for SpendPace in the phone settings, and that the reminder switch in Profile is on. On Android the alert can be a few minutes off the scheduled time.

This is not financial advice.

SpendPace is a budgeting guide. It does not know your bank balance and it is not advice about tax, debt, or investments.
