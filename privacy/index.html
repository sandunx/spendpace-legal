SpendPace Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

SpendPace is a budgeting app developed by Sandun Balasooriya ("we"). It helps you compare what you have spent with how much of the budget period has passed, and it shows a daily amount that is safe to spend. This policy says what the app stores, what it does not send anywhere, and what changes when paid plans are added later.

Short version

  • There is no account and no SpendPace server.
  • What you type stays on your phone.
  • We do not sell data, show ads, or use analytics.
  • Version 1.0 does not include purchases. A later update may add an optional paid plan. That update is described at the end.

What is stored on your phone

SpendPace saves its data in the phone's local storage (on-device storage used only by this app). Nothing in this list is uploaded by version 1.0:

  • The name you optionally type, used in the greeting and in reminder text on your phone
  • The goal and income style you pick during setup
  • Your currency, theme (dark or light), monthly budget, and budget-period dates
  • The day of the month your budget period starts, if you set one
  • Expenses and income: date, amount, category or income source, payment method, an optional note, and an optional Need / Want / Impulse tag
  • Category budget limits
  • Subscriptions you track: name, amount, how often they renew, and the next date
  • Saving goals: name, target, amount saved, and due date
  • Whether each reminder is on or off
  • A couple of on-device flags (for example, whether a tip was dismissed)

We do not ask for your bank login, card number, contacts, photos, or location. Payment method is a label you pick (such as "Cash" or "Debit card"). It is not a card number.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the phone. There is no push-notification server and no device token is sent to us. The app asks for notification permission only if you turn a reminder on. You can turn reminders off in SpendPace or in the phone's settings.

On Android, reminders may arrive a few minutes away from the exact clock time. The app does not request the special "exact alarm" permission.

Exporting a spreadsheet

If you tap Export to CSV, SpendPace writes a spreadsheet file in the app's private cache and opens your phone's share sheet. Where that file goes (Mail, Files, Drive, or somewhere else) is your choice. SpendPace does not upload it. Any service you send it to has its own privacy policy.

Links

If a privacy or terms link is shown in the app, tapping it opens that page in the browser. That is the only network use in version 1.0. The app works offline.

Children

SpendPace is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The optional name stays on the device.

Deleting your data

Profile → Erase everything deletes the transactions, subscriptions, goals, and category budgets stored on that device. Uninstalling the app removes the rest. There is no copy on a server for us to delete.

What we do not do

  • No account, and no sale of data
  • No advertising and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No analytics and no crash-reporting service
  • The purchase library is included in the app install but is not started in version 1.0, so it does not receive data in this version

Paid plans, later

Version 1.0 is free and does not sell anything. A later update may add an optional subscription ("Premium"). When that ships, and only if you choose to subscribe:

  • Apple (on iPhone) or Google (on Android) processes the payment. We never see your card number.
  • A company called RevenueCat tells the app whether the subscription is active. To do that, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app id, which store product was bought, and whether it is currently active. It does not receive your transactions, budgets, notes, or name.
  • You can restore a subscription from the store account you bought it with.
  • The privacy answers on the App Store and Google Play will be updated before that version is released.

Until that update, ignore this section. It describes a feature that is not turned on.

Changes

If this policy changes, the hosted page will show a new effective date. The copy inside the app is the one that was current when that version was built.
